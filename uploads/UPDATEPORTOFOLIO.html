3. DENTALRAVE — UX Case Study (dewasa, BUKAN anak-anak)

Tag: UI/UX DESIGN • UX RESEARCH • USABILITY TESTING

Overview

An end-to-end UX case study for a dental-care app — helping users find trusted dentists, manage appointments, and access reviews. Redesigned from usability testing with 12 participants across age groups.

Role: UX Designer & Researcher (end-to-end) · 7-day design sprint · Tools: Figma

Problem

Finding a trusted dentist and managing dental appointments is fragmented and stressful.

Initial surveys surfaced four core pain points: finding a trusted dentist, appointment

management, communication, and access to reviews.

Research

- User survey to identify and prioritize pain points

- Unmoderated usability testing with 12 participants across age groups

- Findings: users needed stronger color contrast and clearer clinic location detail

Process

- Persona, user story, user journey, HMW, user flow

- Wireframe → high-fidelity mockup → interactive prototype

- Design system + accessibility review

Outcome

Redesigned based on findings: added clinic location selection, nearby hospital info, and a

simplified dentist selection page — producing cleaner navigation and faster feature discovery.

Visuals: 4 pain points · before/after redesign · prototype screens.
