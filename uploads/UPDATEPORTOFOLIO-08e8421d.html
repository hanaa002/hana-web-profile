Title

Pasarin — Price Monitoring Platform for Indonesian Online Sellers

Subtitle

Designing an end-to-end SaaS platform that helps small online sellers monitor competitor prices, receive real-time WhatsApp alerts, and make smarter pricing decisions.

Overview

Instead of manually checking competitor stores every day, sellers can automatically monitor price changes, receive instant notifications, and adjust their pricing strategy through a single platform.

Pasarin is a SaaS price monitoring platform designed specifically for Indonesian small and medium-sized online sellers. The product enables users to track competitor pricing automatically, receive real-time WhatsApp notifications, and gain actionable pricing recommendations without relying on expensive enterprise tools.

This project is an end-to-end UI/UX case study covering market research, competitor analysis, user research, information architecture, wireframing, high-fidelity interface design, and interactive prototyping.

Problem Statement

Indonesia's e-commerce growth continues to fuel a new wave of online sellers, reinforced by digitally-transformed SMEs reporting an average 26% increase in revenue compared to conventional businesses. Yet amid this momentum, many new sellers struggle to sustain their business — often pricing products without real visibility into market and competitor movements, relying on guesswork rather than data.

Existing price monitoring solutions fail to close this gap. Global tools like Price2Spy can cost upwards of $310 per month for full functionality — far beyond the reach of small sellers. Meanwhile, local alternatives are typically built as broad, all-in-one product research tools rather than focused, accessible solutions dedicated to price monitoring alone.

This gap forms the foundation of this product's design: a focused, affordable price-monitoring tool built specifically around the needs and digital habits of small online sellers in Indonesia.

Research & Discovery

To better understand the competitive landscape, I evaluated both international and local price-monitoring platforms, comparing their usability, feature sets, pricing models, and overall user experience.

This competitive analysis revealed four major gaps:

  • Existing products prioritize feature quantity over usability.
  • Global solutions are financially inaccessible for small businesses.
  • Most platforms visualize data without helping users make decisions.
  • None are designed around the daily workflow of Indonesian sellers, particularly WhatsApp as their primary communication channel.

These insights became the foundation for Pasarin's product positioning: a focused, affordable, and actionable price-monitoring platform built specifically for Indonesian online sellers.

User Research

Research focused on understanding the behaviors, frustrations, and decision-making processes of small online sellers operating on Indonesian marketplaces.

The primary persona represents independent sellers with limited capital who require simple tools that minimize manual work while supporting faster pricing decisions.

Research findings highlighted that users value:

  • Clear pricing recommendations over raw analytical data.
  • Instant notifications rather than continuously monitoring dashboards.
  • Fast onboarding with minimal setup.
  • Simple interfaces that prioritize actions instead of overwhelming metrics.

Design Strategy

The product strategy centered on reducing complexity without sacrificing functionality.

Key product decisions included:

  • Three-step onboarding to reduce activation friction.
  • Automated competitor tracking through product URLs.
  • WhatsApp notifications for immediate awareness.
  • AI-assisted pricing recommendations.
  • Simplified dashboard hierarchy for faster decision-making.
  • Two dashboard complexity levels to accommodate different seller segments.

Final Solution

The final product delivers a streamlined SaaS experience that enables sellers to:

  • Monitor competitor prices automatically.
  • Receive real-time WhatsApp alerts.
  • Understand their pricing position instantly.
  • Identify products requiring immediate attention.
  • Make informed pricing decisions without manual competitor analysis.

The interface emphasizes clarity through simplified navigation, focused dashboards, consistent visual hierarchy, and reusable design components, allowing users to complete critical tasks with minimal cognitive effort.

Reflection

This project challenged me to balance product simplicity with feature completeness.

During early iterations, the dashboard became increasingly feature-rich, making the experience overwhelming for first-time users. Rather than removing functionality, I redesigned the product into two dashboard experiences—a lightweight version for solo sellers and a more advanced workspace for growing businesses.

This experience reinforced an important design principle:

Scalable products are not built by adding more features—they are built by delivering the right level of complexity for the right users.
